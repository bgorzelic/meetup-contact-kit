# Event-day checklist

## Before you leave

- [ ] The public HTTPS page loads on cellular data.
- [ ] The printed QR scans from a second phone.
- [ ] The on-screen QR scans at the brightness and distance you expect.
- [ ] The contact card opens and saves the right name, title, email, and URL.
- [ ] Each work link loads and you can explain your own role honestly.
- [ ] Direct email or another fallback works if the page or a form fails.
- [ ] Any form has been tested through actual delivery—not just a success message.
- [ ] Both devices are charged; pack a charger and paper backup.
- [ ] You have permission to put up a sign or tablet at the venue.

## In conversation

- Ask what the person works on. Show one relevant example, not every link.
- Offer the QR when they want your details. A scan is not an opt-in.
- Ask whether they want a follow-up and what would be useful to send.
- Keep any private notes off the public page.

## Afterward

- Follow up individually with people who agreed to it.
- Refer to the actual conversation and one useful next step.
- Do not import attendees or public profiles into a bulk outreach list.
- Record what worked and what confused people before adapting the kit for the next event.
